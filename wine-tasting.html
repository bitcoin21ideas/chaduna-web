Georgian Wine Tasting

Immerse yourself in the rich history of the Cradle of Wine. Join us for a curated journey through Georgia's unique regions, technologies, and flavors.

Practical info

  • Price: 70 GEL per person
  • Duration: 1.5–2 hours
  • Booking lead time: preferably 24 hours in advance
  • Cancellation: free – please cancel if you changed your mind
  • Meeting point: inside Chaduna cafe & wine bar
  • Group size: minimum 2 people, please contact us for large group bookings.

What’s included

Tasting of 5 distinct wines

Expect a mix of styles including amber, natural, and qvevri-aged wines. We pour a representative selection from key Georgian regions. Each lineup is curated by our team based on season, availability, and balance across styles.

History of Georgian winemaking

Georgia is often called the Cradle of Wine, with an 8,000-year winemaking tradition. You’ll get the context behind how wine became part of everyday Georgian culture, and what makes local varieties so distinctive. We keep it approachable and interactive, not academic.

Guide to regions & Qvevri technology

You’ll learn what “qvevri” means and how this method shapes texture, tannin, and aroma. We’ll compare regions and styles so you can recognize what changes from Kakheti to Imereti and beyond. You’ll leave with a practical way to read a Georgian wine list.

Tasting technique & palate guidance

We’ll walk through how to taste, what to look for, and how to describe what you like (even if you’re not sure yet). The goal is to help you find your preferences and feel confident ordering Georgian wine afterward. Questions are always welcome throughout.

FAQ

Do I need to know anything about wine beforehand?

No – this tasting is designed for beginners and curious drinkers. We’ll guide you through how to taste, how to describe what you like, and what makes Georgian wine unique.

Is the tasting available in English/Russian?

Yes. We can host the tasting in English, Russian, or Georgian – please mention your preferred language when booking.

Can I book for a private group?

Yes. Private group tastings are available on request, subject to availability and group size.

What wines are included?

We taste 5 wines with a mix of styles (including amber, natural, and qvevri-aged), selected by our team based on what’s in great form that week. The lineup typically represents different Georgian regions and approaches.

Is food served during the tasting?

This is a wine-focused experience. If you’d like to add food, you’re welcome to order from our menu.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel if you changed your mind – all cancellations are free.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance so we can schedule your tasting and prepare the wines.
