Breakfast in Old Tbilisi

Start your day in the heart of historic Old Tbilisi at Chaduna. We offer a cozy, laptop-friendly atmosphere perfect for enjoying breakfast at your own pace. With our full all-day breakfast menu, you can savor our signature dishes, including our famous syrniki and the local favorite Chizhi Bizhi, whenever the mood strikes.

Menu Highlights

Chizhi Bizhi

A beloved Georgian classic similar to shakshuka, featuring eggs, tomatoes, onions, cheese, and a unique blend of spices and herbs. It's a local specialty you won't want to miss.

Syrniki

Our most famous dish: light and fluffy cottage cheese pancakes served with seasonal fruits.

Quesadillas

Available in Chicken or Veggie varieties, packed with a bell pepper mix, tomato, and mozzarella.

Bruschetta Selection

Choose from a variety of toppings like pesto, champignon, or bacon and apple, all served on toasted baguette.
