Winyl Sessions

Vinyl and Wine

At Chaduna, music isn't just something that fills the room – it's one of the main reasons we’re here. Winyl Sessions is a monthly gathering where we move the needle from passive hearing to active listening.

The Sound

Pure analog playback from our private vinyl collection, including rare pressings and B-sides.


The Story

Insider commentary from our founder, Duka, drawing on his years in the music industry to break down production, lore, and legacy.


The Sip

A curated environment where the wine in your glass is as complex as the tracks on the turntable.

We built this for the crate-diggers, the audiophiles, and the curious. Whether you’re a lifelong fan or discovering an artist for the first time, Winyl Sessions is about the shared experience of sound.

Digital goods, early tracklists, and community discussion.
