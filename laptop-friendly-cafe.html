FAQ

Can I use my laptop in the cafe?

Yes, you are welcome to use your laptop in the cafe. However, for extended work sessions or video calls, we strongly recommend our dedicated .hub coworking space to ensure a comfortable experience for all guests.

Are there meeting rooms?

While we do not offer dedicated meeting rooms, our coworking space features flexible work zones designed to accommodate various work needs.
